How to play

Drag a crate where you want it, round corners too: one drag is one move. Rocks, logs and other crates stop it.
Push each crate out through the gate of its colour. The flags show it; its camp spot waits outside.
Every move brings the night closer. Get the camp ready before dark: the fewer moves, the more stars.
